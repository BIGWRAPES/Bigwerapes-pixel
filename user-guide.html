User Guide

BigWrapes Pixel helps students showcase their businesses and connect with potential customers. Visitors can discover businesses, explore their profiles, and contact owners directly.

A discovery and showcase platform

BigWrapes Pixel is not a checkout marketplace. Customers and business owners arrange communication, purchases, and transactions directly with one another.

01

Getting Started

You can browse public business listings without an account. An account is needed for features such as submitting a business.

  1. Choose an account type. Create a student account or register as a business owner.
  2. Confirm your account. Follow any email-confirmation instructions shown during registration.
  3. Sign in. Use the email and password you registered with.
  4. Open the relevant page. Browse the directory or go to “List a Business” to submit a profile.

02

Browsing Businesses

Open the Business Directory to browse student-run businesses. Use the available category filters to narrow the listings, then choose “View Business” to open a profile.

Profiles may include a business description, cover image, product or service photos, location, and contact or social links supplied by the owner.

  • Fashion & Accessories
  • Digital Services
  • Crafts & Handmade
  • Photography & Media

03

Creating a Business

Sign in, open List a Business, and complete the submission form. Add clear, accurate details that help visitors understand what the business offers.

  • A public display name and short owner bio
  • Business name, category, and description
  • A cover image and 1 to 15 product or service images

Images must be JPG, PNG, or WebP and no larger than 5 MB each. Only upload images you own or have permission to use. Only publish contact or location details you are comfortable making public.

04

Managing Your Business

After signing in, return to List a Business to update your public owner profile and manage product photos. You can add photos or remove individual photos from your listings.

Current availability
Editing a published business’s name, category, description, and contact fields is not available in the owner interface yet. Keep this in mind before submitting your listing.

Review the public profile after publishing to make sure its information and images look right to visitors.

05

Contacting Businesses

Use the contact options shown on a business profile. Depending on what the owner has supplied, this may include WhatsApp, phone, email, or social media.

BigWrapes Pixel connects visitors with business owners; messages, purchases, and other arrangements happen directly between them.

06

Sharing

Each public business profile has a shareable link. Use “Share Business” to open your device’s sharing options or “Copy Link” to copy the URL. You can then send it through WhatsApp, social media, messaging apps, or email.

A simple message: Check out my business on BigWrapes Pixel and see what I offer.

07

Account & Privacy

  • Keep your password private and use one that is difficult to guess.
  • Only publish information you agree to make publicly visible.
  • Do not put passwords or sensitive personal information in a business description or public contact field.
  • If you suspect someone accessed your account, change your password and contact the platform for help.

08

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BigWrapes Pixel an online shopping website?

No. It is a business discovery and showcase platform. Customers contact owners to make arrangements directly.

Do I need an account to browse businesses?

No account is needed to browse public listings. An account is required for restricted features such as submitting a business.

Who can list a business?

The platform is intended for students and student entrepreneurs who want to showcase a business.

Can I edit a business after submitting it?

The owner interface currently supports public owner-profile updates and product-photo management. Editing a published business’s core details is not available yet.

Where can I get help?

Use the Contact page for questions or technical problems. Include the page you were using and what happened.
